# Batch A · Four website themes

Mode: Persuade for home; Operate for the sample-driven design gallery. Primary target: theme-system.css. Related: scripts/build-theme-preview.mjs, site-theme.js, scripts/build-design-gallery.mjs, design-preview.js.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A birthplace becomes a story worth keeping. Replace the old text-led introduction with an original scenic world, a working country-start form and actual collectible specimens. All four visual worlds share business behavior.

OWN-WORLD: Clear-blue lake light is the default; warm travel paper, spacious editorial and navy/gold cinema are peer themes. Display lettering is locally hosted Bodoni Moda; controls use readable local sans. Theme tokens are separate from old CSS and printed keepsake palettes.

STORY: A newcomer understands fictional life simulation, starts randomly or in a chosen country, and discovers completed samples and keepsakes. Returning players can continue without overwriting a life. Core content stays expanded.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop pairs large readable title and real actions with actual card specimens over original landscape; mobile prioritizes offer and start, then specimens. Theme, locale and a usable mobile navigation remain visible and distinct.

FORM: Code-led execution of user-pinned four reference images; no competing identity selection. One semantic home structure with material, typography and composition variations. Six clearly labelled sample-driven prototypes define future core views without pretending batch B/C is done.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.

## Boundary

Local opt-in THEME_PREVIEW build only, no deployment. Existing 226 URLs, nine locales, calendar, RNG, local saves, free exports and Stripe sandbox authorization stay compatible. A05 prototypes may demonstrate completed public samples; production gameplay/studio redesign belongs to batch B. Never call a generic image plus labels six finished interaction studies.
