## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

1in8billion.live is a free independent browser life simulator, with country-weight tools, practical guides and four supported public languages. Visitors can start a fictional life, make or automate choices, keep local progress and save/share compatible results.

The current requested outcome is visitor retention: help people begin, follow a life through meaningful moments, replay, share and return. This is the user's clarified meaning of 留客, not a request primarily about empty states.

## Users

Current product supports English, Japanese, Korean and Traditional Chinese readers on phones and desktop browsers. Specific market segments beyond these existing audiences remain an open research question. Do not invent measured demographic segments or demand.

## Operating Context

Vanilla HTML/CSS/JavaScript game, Node static build, existing Git-to-Cloudflare deployment. Production baseline v1.4.0, revision 00b216917f7dbd116fe166e4582e6bcdb8fa34a4, with 62 intended indexable routes. Discovery/indexing and retention are measured separately.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve existing URLs, GA4 identity, default collection behavior, source disclosures and MIT attribution.
- Preserve replay-v1 streams, numeric model weights, legacy localStorage keys and compatible saved lives.
- Local continuation, PNG results and deterministic automatic replay links are separate capabilities; there is no account or cloud-save service.
- Inherited country inputs have unverified observation years. A simulation is fictional and cannot predict an individual's real life.
- New visual/audio presentation must not change simulation randomness or claim statistical validity.
- Original graphics and licensed/original music are required. Competitor assets and publication features are reference evidence, not granted rights or existing capabilities here.

## Brand Commitments

User confirmed on 20 October 2026: retain the blue brand and add warm original pixel scenes to the life experience. Existing brand name and independent-project identity remain.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing game and build source, 12 actual reproducible sample proofs, local/production acceptance reports, original UI screenshots, country provenance and the user's reference screenshot. GA4 early realtime observations are not a clean organic-retention baseline. Competitor public page and the supplied screenshot inform the proposed experience; competitor motion/audio quality has not been fully tested.

## Product Principles

1. A first session offers a clear, playable fictional life without mandatory registration.
2. Narrative and visual detail must come from that life's actual events and state.
3. Local/private records stay local unless the player deliberately chooses a supported sharing action.
4. Readable controls, optional audio and reduced motion support the experience.
5. Improvements are evaluated with comparable user/session cohorts, not invented popularity or unrelated event ratios.
