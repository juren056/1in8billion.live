## Measurement rules

Use one truthful placement per relevant community, not bulk repeated posts. Log publication URL, date, moderation state and referral visits only after an actual publication. Compare source/medium sessions and same-session start/completion cohorts after processing; a clipboard copy is not an inbound visit. Player-generated replay links use player/share/replay UTM labels, with the replay seed still only in the fragment and excluded from GA4 page_location. Internal site links receive no marketing UTM, so they do not overwrite acquisition attribution.

Review checkpoints after a clean release interval: 48-hour technical availability; 7-day queries/pages/country/device; 14-day indexing by content cluster; 30-day attributable acquisition and start/completion/share funnels. These are an operating checklist, not scheduled automations.
