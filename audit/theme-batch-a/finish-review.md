## 2. Fidelity and craft assessment

The implementation preserves the blue brand, makes the product and its free browser model understandable, uses real keepsake specimens rather than invented premium claims, retains the expanded homepage content, and supplies four distinct scenic assets. The journal imagery captures paper/travel material; the editorial art has bright architectural space; the night art provides an appropriate warm dusk setting. These are meaningful asset changes.

However, the four first viewports still share almost exactly the same copy/start/form/cards topology. The references and A04 ask for distinct compositions as well as materials. Small grid-ratio, border and card-rotation differences do not yet fulfill that promise. More seriously, the night theme loses readable foregrounds across most of the page. Mobile navigation is visibly crowded despite the automated no-overflow result. The six-scene gallery has tab switching and event stepping, but several tabs are content placeholders rather than usable scene studies.
